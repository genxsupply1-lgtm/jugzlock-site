FAQ

Which JUGZLOCK do I need?

Standard fits traditional one-gallon jugs like Poland Spring and milk-jug-style bottles. The UA Edition is sized for the Under Armour 1-gallon water jug and also takes the 5 7/8" and 6 1/2" round inserts.

Will it fit my vehicle?

JUGZLOCK sits on a flat surface like a console, floor or seat. For a raised mount, the JUGZLOCK Dock plugs into two cup holders using slip-on sleeves sized for your cup holders.

How long does shipping take?

Every JUGZLOCK is custom-made to order. Please allow up to 5 business days before it ships, plus delivery time. Shipping within the US is free.

Can I get a different color?

Yes — choose “Custom Color (+$5)” on any product and we'll reach out to confirm your color.

What is it made of?

JUGZLOCK is 3D-printed in our shop, designed for function and durability. Avoid leaving it in direct sun in a closed vehicle for long periods.

Is it safe to use while driving?

JUGZLOCK helps keep your jug from tipping and sliding, but it is not a substitute for properly securing loose items. Only drink or adjust your jug when safely parked.
